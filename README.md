# 📊 Probability & Statistics — Interview & Viva Preparation

A structured collection of **Probability and Statistics questions with easy-to-understand answers**, designed for **interview preparation, viva revision, and Machine Learning fundamentals**.

This repository covers the important concepts of probability, random variables, distributions, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, correlation, regression, and statistics used in Machine Learning.

* Addition and Multiplication Rules

* Law of Total Probability

* Mean vs Median
* Range

* Percentiles and Quartiles

* Skewness
* Kurtosis

### 4. Sampling & Central Limit Theorem

* Population vs Sample
* Sampling and its Importance
* Sampling Methods
* Sampling Bias

### 5. Estimation & Confidence Intervals

* Point Estimates

### 6. Hypothesis Testing

### 7. Common Statistical Tests

* T-test

### 9. Statistics for Machine Learning
