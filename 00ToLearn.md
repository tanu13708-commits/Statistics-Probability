# 00 — To Learn

## Recommended Learning Order

### Step 1 — Probability
Understand outcomes, events, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, and probability rules.

### Step 2 — Random Variables & Distributions
Learn discrete and continuous random variables, PMF, PDF, CDF, expectation, variance, and common distributions.

### Step 3 — Descriptive Statistics
Learn how to summarize data using mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation, quartiles, skewness, and kurtosis.

### Step 4 — Sampling & CLT
Understand populations, samples, sampling methods, sampling bias, and the Central Limit Theorem.

### Step 5 — Estimation
Learn point estimates, confidence intervals, standard error, and interpretation of confidence levels.

### Step 6 — Hypothesis Testing
Learn hypotheses, significance level, p-value, statistical significance, errors, power, and one/two-tailed tests.

### Step 7 — Statistical Tests
Understand when to use z-test, t-test, ANOVA, chi-square, parametric, and non-parametric tests.

### Step 8 — Correlation & Regression
Understand covariance, correlation, Pearson/Spearman correlation, causation, and linear regression.

### Step 9 — Statistics for ML
Apply statistical thinking to outliers, scaling, data leakage, distributions, and model evaluation.

## Study Tip

For every question, try to answer in this order:

**Definition → Formula (if needed) → Simple example → When/why it is useful**

This makes the answer suitable for both viva and technical interviews.
