## Most Important Formulas

### Probability

**P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)**

**P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B|A)**

**P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)**

### Bayes' Theorem

**P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A) / P(B)**

### Variance

**Var(X) = E[X²] - (E[X])²**

### Standard Deviation

**SD = √Variance**

### IQR

**IQR = Q3 - Q1**

### Standard Error of Mean

**SE = s / √n**

### Standardization

**z = (x - μ) / σ**

### Min-Max Scaling

**x' = (x - min) / (max - min)**

### Statistical Power

**Power = 1 - β**

## Final Interview Strategy

For a conceptual question:

**1. Give the definition.**

**2. State the important formula if applicable.**

**3. Give a small example.**

**4. Explain when it is useful.**

For ML-related questions, always connect the statistical concept to:
- Data analysis
- Preprocessing
- Model evaluation
- Uncertainty
- Experimental design

This helps demonstrate conceptual understanding rather than memorization.
